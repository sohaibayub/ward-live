About the census

Between June and July 2026 WARD audited the homepages of 161,220 civil society organisations with a recorded website in the registers of the United Kingdom, Australia, the United States, New Zealand and Ireland. 135,604 could be reached. The distribution shown above comes from those records, released as an open dataset (Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23529469). Static analysis detects a subset of real violations, so counts are floors; the browser-based validation in the dataset quantifies the floor per criterion.

    Help and contact

    WARD is a static-analysis checker. It fetches the HTML of one page, does not run scripts, and evaluates 18 WCAG 2.1 criteria plus the four criteria WCAG 2.2 introduces. It does not test colour contrast or anything that requires rendering. A clean result here means the page passes the machine-checkable floor, not the whole standard.

    Code and data: cso-web-accessibility repository on GitHub. Contact: Muhammad Sohaib Ayub, Institute for Data and AI Research, University of Galway.
